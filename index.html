Democrats projected to win control of the House and Senate

Counting continues in uncalled races. Seat totals reflect AP race calls as reported by news outlets; see sources.

House

Democrats Projected to Win the House (media projection, unofficial)

The AP projects Democrats will win the House majority, with Democrats at 253 called seats and Republicans at 155, and 27 races still uncalled.

Senate

Democrats Projected to Win the Senate (media projection, unofficial)

Democrats are projected to win Senate control, holding 54 seats to Republicans' 44, with 33 of 35 races called. They flipped seats in North Carolina, South Carolina, Ohio, Texas, Kansas, Iowa and Maine, and NBC shows the same 54 to 44 count.

Race calls and seat counts: AP (via The Guardian). Cross-checked with NBC/CBS/CNN/NYT where noted.

How we call races: a Senate or Governor race on the map is marked called only when AP has projected it and we have cross-checked it with a second major outlet (NBC, CBS, CNN or NYT). Anything not yet confirmed stays purple (uncalled). House seat totals are AP’s overall count.

    About the Desk

    The Election Desk tracks the 2026 midterm results race by race, using AP results as the master count. A race shows a winner only when the AP has projected it and a second major outlet (NBC, CBS, CNN or NYT) agrees, and races without a call are marked as uncalled with no projected winner. When outlets report different totals, we say so and show both. We report the numbers, not opinions.

    The Election Desk is an independent tracker. Sources: The Associated Press (race calls), NBC News, NPR, The Guardian, BBC, Reuters, Anadolu Agency, Wikipedia election pages, and other outlets linked per item. Times are US Central (CT). Map data: Natural Earth via world-atlas / us-atlas (ISC); rendering by D3 (ISC). Embedded for offline use.

    The Election Desk is an independent hobby project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Associated Press, NBC News, The Guardian, or any other news outlet, government agency, party, or campaign. These are NOT official results. Race calls are media projections, and official results are certified by state and local election authorities. For official results and voting information, check your state or county election office or vote.gov. Provided for information only, as is, with no warranty of accuracy or completeness.
