## How they vote

Iran elects its president by a direct two-round popular vote. The supreme leader is chosen indirectly by the 88-member Assembly of Experts, which on 8 March 2026 named Mojtaba Khamenei supreme leader. The latest presidential election was a snap election after President Raisi died in a May 2024 helicopter crash, with rounds on 28 June and 5 July 2024. Officially reported first-round turnout was about 40%, a record low, and runoff turnout was 49.8%. Masoud Pezeshkian defeated Saeed Jalili in the runoff, according to official results reported by RFE/RL.
