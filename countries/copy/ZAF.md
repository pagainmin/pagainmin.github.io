## How they vote

South Africa's 2024 general election, held on 29 May 2024, chose the 400-member National Assembly by closed-list proportional representation. Voting is not compulsory. Turnout was 58.64% of registered voters, according to the Electoral Commission (IEC). The African National Congress (ANC) won 159 seats, followed by the Democratic Alliance with 87, uMkhonto weSizwe with 58, the Economic Freedom Fighters with 39, and the Inkatha Freedom Party with 17, according to the Parliamentary Monitoring Group.

## Wild facts

- Voters used three ballots for the first time, because independent candidates could stand. (https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sun-sets-2024-elections)
- The ANC lost its parliamentary majority for the first time since 1994. (https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/6/2/south-africa-elections-results-what-happens-next)
