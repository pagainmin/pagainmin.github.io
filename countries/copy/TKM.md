## How they vote

Turkmenistan's president is elected by direct vote for a seven-year term, with no term limits. In the early presidential election of 12 March 2022, nine candidates ran and 3,362,052 of 3,460,080 registered voters cast ballots, an officially reported turnout of 97.17%. Serdar Berdimuhamedov was officially declared the winner, succeeding his father, Gurbanguly Berdimuhamedov. OSCE/ODIHR noted a lack of political pluralism and deployed no observation mission.

## Wild facts

- The election passed power from President Gurbanguly Berdimuhamedov to his son Serdar, after he called for a handover to "young leaders." (https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/f/documents/3/2/513565.pdf)
- Officially, 9 candidates ran and 3,362,052 of 3,460,080 registered voters cast ballots. (https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/en/post/61563/results-presidential-elections)
