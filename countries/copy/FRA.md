## How they vote

France's most recent legislative election, a snap election held in two rounds on 30 June and 7 July 2024, chose the 577 members of the National Assembly. Each member represents a single-member constituency under a two-round system. Voting is not compulsory. Turnout was 66.7%, with 32,910,888 voters out of 49,332,761 registered, according to IPU Parline. According to IPU, the New Popular Front won 180 seats, the presidential camp Ensemble 163, the National Rally 125, and The Republicans 39.

## Wild facts

- First-round turnout of 66.7% was the highest for a parliamentary first round since 1997. (https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx82n333ze7o)
- To be elected in the first round, a candidate needs an absolute majority of votes cast and the votes of at least a quarter of registered voters. (https://data.ipu.org/parliament/FR/FR-LC01/election/FR-LC01-E20240630)
