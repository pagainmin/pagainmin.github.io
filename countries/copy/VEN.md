## How they vote

Venezuela elects its president in a single-round plurality vote. Voting is electronic, with paper tally sheets (actas), and it is not compulsory. In the presidential election of 28 July 2024, Nicolás Maduro was declared the winner over Edmundo González, with turnout of 59.97%, officially reported (CNE); the result is disputed. The UN Panel of Experts noted that no polling-station results were published. According to the Carter Center, the opposition's tally sheets showed González winning. In January 2026, US forces seized Maduro, and Delcy Rodríguez became interim president. No new election date has been set.

## Wild facts

- Opposition poll watchers collected tally sheets (actas) from 80% of precincts, and they showed González winning, according to the Carter Center. (https://www.cartercenter.org/stories/center-finds-democracy-thwarted-in-venezuela/)
- The Carter Center said the election did not meet international standards and that it could not verify the CNE result. (https://www.cartercenter.org/news/venezuela-073024/)
- US forces seized Maduro in January 2026, and Delcy Rodríguez became interim president. (https://www.semafor.com/article/11/02/2026/elections-pose-next-test-for-venezuela)
