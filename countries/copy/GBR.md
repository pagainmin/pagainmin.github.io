## How they vote

The United Kingdom's 2024 general election, held on 4 July 2024, elected all 650 members of the House of Commons. Each member represents a single-member constituency and is chosen by first-past-the-post, so the candidate with the most votes wins. Voting is not compulsory. Turnout was 59.7%, down from 67.3% in 2019 (House of Commons Library, briefing CBP-8749). Labour won 411 seats, the Conservatives 121, and the Liberal Democrats 72, according to the UK Parliament's official results.

## Wild facts

- Labour won 411 of the 650 seats, a majority. (https://electionresults.parliament.uk/general-elections/6/)
