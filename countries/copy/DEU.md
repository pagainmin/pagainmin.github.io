## How they vote

Germany's Bundestag election on 23 February 2025 used a mixed-member proportional system for 630 seats. Each voter casts a first vote for a constituency candidate and a second vote for a party list. Seats are allocated by the Sainte-Laguë/Schepers method, and parties generally need 5% of second votes to win list seats. Voting is not compulsory. Turnout was 82.5%, according to the Federal Returning Officer. The CDU won 164 seats and the CSU 44, followed by the AfD with 152, the SPD with 120, the Greens with 85, and Die Linke with 64.

## Wild facts

- The BSW party narrowly missed the 5% threshold. (https://bundeswahlleiterin.de/en/bundestagswahlen/2025/ergebnisse/bund-99.html)
- The SSW, the party of the Danish and Frisian minority, is exempt from the 5% threshold. (https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2025/kw11-bundeswahlausschuss-1056272)
- The Bundestag shrank to 630 seats from 735. (https://bundeswahlleiterin.de/en/info/presse/mitteilungen/bundestagswahl-2025/29_25_endgueltiges-ergebnis.html)
- Official statistics sample real ballots, about 1.6 million voters in 2025, to analyse turnout by age and sex. (https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2025/06/PD25_223_14.html)
