## How they vote

Belarus elects its president by direct vote, and the ballot includes an "against all" option. In the presidential election of 26 January 2025, 5,916,195 of 6,903,994 registered voters took part, a turnout of 85.69%. Alexander Lukashenko won, officially reported (CEC). Officially, 2,887,052 people voted early, 41.8% of registered voters. The Belarusian Helsinki Committee says early voting was coerced. OSCE/ODIHR did not observe the election because it was not invited in time.

## Wild facts

- Photographing ballots was banned. (https://belhelcom.org/en/document/elections2025-preliminary-analytical-report-results-observation-elections-president)
- It was the third election since 2020 that OSCE/ODIHR could not observe, because it was not invited in time. (https://odihr.osce.org/odihr/elections/584445)
