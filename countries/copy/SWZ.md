## How they vote

Eswatini is an absolute monarchy, with King Mswati III as head of state. Under the tinkhundla system, political parties are banned from elections, and candidates stand as individuals by constituency. In the latest House of Assembly election, held on 29 September 2023, voters elected 59 members. According to the SADC observer mission, 584,710 voters registered, 91.2% of the eligible population, and the vote was peaceful.

## Wild facts

- Political parties are banned from elections, so every candidate runs as an individual. (https://data.ipu.org/parliament/SZ/SZ-LC01/election/SZ-LC01-E20230929)
- 584,710 voters registered, 91.2% of the eligible population. (https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Eswatini%20SEOM%202023%20PRELIMINARY%20STATEMENT%20-%201st%20October%202023.pdf)
- SADC observers called the vote peaceful and recommended dialogue on the role of political parties. (https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Eswatini%20SEOM%202023%20PRELIMINARY%20STATEMENT%20-%201st%20October%202023.pdf)
