## How they vote

Japan's latest House of Representatives election was a snap election held on 8 February 2026, for 465 seats. The chamber is elected under a parallel system that combines single-member districts with proportional representation blocks. Voting is not compulsory. Turnout was 56.3% on the proportional ballot, with 58,061,448 voters, according to IPU Parline. The Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) won 316 of the 465 seats, according to CSIS, a two-thirds majority, and the Japan Innovation Party (Ishin) won 36.

## Wild facts

- The LDP won 316 of 465 seats, the largest two-thirds majority won by a single party in the postwar era, according to CSIS. (https://www.csis.org/analysis/takaichi-dominates-japans-lower-house-election)
