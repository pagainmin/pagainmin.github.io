## How they vote

India's 2024 general election chose the 543 members of the Lok Sabha by first-past-the-post, with each member representing a single-member constituency. The Election Commission of India (ECI) counted about 97.98 crore (roughly 980 million) electors, including 48,272 third-gender electors, voting at 1,052,664 polling stations. Turnout was 66.1% including postal ballots (65.66% excluding them), according to the ECI's statistical highlights; a widely reported 65.79% was a provisional figure. In the final results, the BJP won 240 seats, short of the 272 needed for a majority, and the Indian National Congress won 99.

## Wild facts

- The Surat seat was decided without a vote, because its candidate was elected unopposed. (https://www.eci.gov.in/EBooks/atlas-2024/files/basic-html/page27.html)
- Karur in Tamil Nadu had 54 candidates on the ballot, and Dhubri in Assam had the highest turnout, at 92.30%. (https://www.eci.gov.in/EBooks/atlas-2024/files/basic-html/page27.html)
- The highest polling station was Tashigang in Himachal Pradesh, at 15,256 feet. (https://www.eci.gov.in/EBooks/atlas-2024/files/basic-html/page27.html)
- The indelible ink used to mark voters' fingers comes from a single manufacturer, Mysore Paints & Varnish Ltd. (https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-politics/lok-sabha-elections-voting-indelible-ink-9279884/)
