## How they vote

Russia's president is elected by a direct two-round vote. The 450-member State Duma uses a mixed system: 225 seats by party-list proportional representation with a 5% threshold, and 225 in single-member districts. In the March 2024 presidential election, turnout was 77.49% and Vladimir Putin won, officially reported (CEC). In the Duma election of 18 to 20 September 2026, turnout was 59.8% and United Russia won the party-list vote, officially reported (TASS). Russia did not invite OSCE/ODIHR observers to the 2024 presidential election.

## Wild facts

- More than 7 million people voted online in the 2026 Duma election, officially reported (TASS/CEC). (https://tass.com/politics/2192899)
- The independent monitor Golos reported voters being made to prove how they voted with photos, screenshots or geolocation. (https://golosinfo.org/en/articles/146797)
- Russia did not invite OSCE/ODIHR observers to the 2024 presidential election. (https://odihr.osce.org/odihr/elections/russia/562065)
