## How they vote

Brazil elects its president by a two-round popular vote, with an absolute majority required to win. Voting is compulsory for literate citizens aged 18 to 70 and voluntary for illiterate citizens, 16- and 17-year-olds, and people over 70, according to International IDEA. In the 2026 election, Flávio Bolsonaro led the first round on 4 October, according to BBC Brasil. In the runoff on 25 October 2026, Lula won, according to the electoral court (TSE). Turnout in the runoff was 80.78%, according to TSE results.

## Wild facts

- Lula is the first president since Brazil's 1985 return to civilian rule to win after losing the first round. (https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckm23y3mg1yyo)
- A court extended voting nationwide by two hours on runoff day. (https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckm23y3mg1yyo)
- The winner was mathematically certain on election night, according to the TSE. (https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/eleicoes-2026-painel-registra-manifestacoes-e-saudacoes-de-mais-de-cem-paises-apos-2o-turno)
