## How they vote

Saudi Arabia is an absolute monarchy and holds no national elections. Elections are held only for municipal councils, which are partly elected. The latest were municipal council elections on 12 December 2015, the first in which women could vote and stand as candidates. According to the BBC, 978 women registered as candidates, and about 130,000 women registered to vote, compared with 1.35 million men. Women were elected to councils for the first time.
