## How they vote

China's president is chosen indirectly: the National People's Congress (NPC) elects the president, and there is no nationwide popular turnout figure. On 10 March 2023, the 14th NPC elected Xi Jinping to a third term. According to The Hindu, all 2,952 deputies present voted for him, with no abstentions or opposing votes, an officially reported result of 2,952 to 0. Xi's third term followed the NPC's removal of the two-term limit five years earlier.
