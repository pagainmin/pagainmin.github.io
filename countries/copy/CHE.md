## How they vote

Switzerland's latest federal election, on 22 October 2023, chose the 200-member National Council by proportional representation by canton, using open lists. The 46 members of the Council of States are elected in each canton, and the 2023 run-offs concluded on 19 November 2023. Swiss voters also decide frequent national referendums. Voting is not compulsory at the federal level; the canton of Schaffhausen is the exception. Turnout for the National Council was 46.7%, with 2,604,984 voters out of 5,583,221 registered, according to IPU Parline.

## Wild facts

- Schaffhausen is the only canton where voting is compulsory: residents up to age 65 who don't vote pay a CHF 6 fine. (https://www.stadt-schaffhausen.ch/abstimmungen/informationen)
- Glarus and Appenzell Innerrhoden still decide cantonal matters at the Landsgemeinde, an open-air assembly. (https://www.ch-info.swiss/en/direkte-demokratie/fakten)
