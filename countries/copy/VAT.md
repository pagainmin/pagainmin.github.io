## How they vote

Vatican City has no popular elections. Its head of state, the pope, is elected by the College of Cardinals meeting in a conclave, and a two-thirds majority of cardinal electors is required. At the most recent conclave, which concluded on 8 May 2025, 133 cardinal electors took part and 89 votes were needed. Robert Prevost was elected on the fourth ballot and took the name Leo XIV, the 267th Bishop of Rome. Individual vote counts are secret. A conclave is held only when the papacy falls vacant.
