## How they vote

North Korea's latest national election, for the Supreme People's Assembly (SPA), was held on 15 March 2026, two years late, with no reason officially given. Each constituency has a single approved candidate, and voters approve or reject that candidate. The election filled all 687 SPA deputies. State media (KCNA) officially reported turnout of 99.99%, as relayed by IPU Parline and Yonhap. No independent turnout estimate exists. Yonhap describes the vote as largely a formality and non-secret.

## Wild facts

- Officially reported (KCNA) turnout in 2026 was 99.99%. (https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20260317000652315)
- The November 2023 local elections were the first time state media reported any votes against candidates, according to Yonhap. (https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20231128001500315)
- The 2026 election was held two years late, and no reason was officially given. (https://data.ipu.org/parliament/KP/KP-LC01/election/KP-LC01-E20260315)
